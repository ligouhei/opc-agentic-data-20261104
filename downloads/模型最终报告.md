## Recipe

The full recipe uses `global_bias_nm: -10`, no component bias or corner serif, and two local +10 nm end-band adjustments at `[596,268,941,288]` and `[596,1248,941,1268]`. The uniform correction removes the measured long-side overprint; the local end bands restore the two window-crossing ends.

## Image inspection

The actual printed images for all five conditions show the two long sides close to the target, with straight middle sidewalls and unchanged nominal width intent. The end bands preserve endpoint placement and do not introduce splits, extra shapes, or visible blank-area damage. Corner rounding remains the principal residual underprint contributor.

This report describes actual forward-model observations only. It makes no wafer or foundry-qualification claim.
